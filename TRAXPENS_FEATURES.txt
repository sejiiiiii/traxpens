==================================================
TRAXPENS — WHAT IT IS & ALL FEATURES
==================================================

WHAT IS TRAXPENS?
Traxpens is a simple, stress-free personal finance app built for salary earners. Instead of complicated spreadsheets, it divides your monthly paycheck into clear buckets and gives you a dynamic daily spending allowance so you never run out of money before payday.

==================================================
ALL FEATURES (UPDATED VERSION)
==================================================

1. PAYCHECK-FIRST BUDGETING
• Monthly Salary Setup: Enter your take-home pay to anchor your entire month's finances.
• Custom Payday Cycle: Pick your salary date (1st, 5th, 28th, etc.) with an automatic live countdown of remaining days.
• 50/30/20 Smart Split: Automatically splits your salary into:
  - 50% Bills & Essentials (rent, groceries, bills, commute)
  - 30% Fun & Personal (dining out, shopping, hobbies)
  - 20% Savings & Investments (emergency fund, SIP, mutual funds)
• 1-Tap Auto-Balance: Customize category percentage sliders anytime and auto-balance back to 100%.

2. DYNAMIC DAILY SPENDING CAP
• Auto-Recalculating Allowance: Calculates your daily spending limit every morning (Remaining Fun Money ÷ Days Left).
• Color-Coded Status: Real-time visual feedback: Green (Safe), Amber (Warning), or Red (Exceeded).
• Weekend Boost (+50%): Automatically gives you a higher allowance on Friday–Sunday while trimming weekdays.
• Event & Trip Locking: Set a custom daily budget for vacations or short trips without ruining your monthly plan.
• Fixed Limit Option: Switch from dynamic auto-cap to a custom fixed daily limit whenever desired.

3. 2-TAP QUICK EXPENSE LOGGING
• 5-Second Entry: Tap the floating '+' button or hit 'N' on desktop to log expenses instantly.
• Quick-Add Presets: One-tap quick buttons for common expenses (Chai, Lunch, Groceries, Auto, Dinner).
• 3 Simple Buckets: Tag transactions as Bills, Fun, or Savings with one tap.

4. EXPENSE FEED & MONTHLY CALENDAR
• Chronological Feed: Complete list of all transactions with instant category filters (All, Bills, Fun, Savings).
• Calendar Heatmap: View monthly spending by date with color badges for days you stayed within or went over limit.
• Digital E-Passbook: Click any date on the calendar to see all transactions, print them, or download an official PDF passbook.

5. SURPLUS & CAPITAL INFLOW ENGINE (NEW)
• Pump Extra Money: Add non-salary income anytime (freelance, bonuses, gifts, refunds, or previous month's leftover cash).
• Multi-Bucket Routing: Direct extra money into Bills, Fun, or Savings.
• Sinking Fund Lock: Lock extra cash for dedicated events (e.g. festivals or travel) so it doesn't inflate your daily spending cap.
• Inflows Manager: Track, edit, and manage all active extra money boosters in one place.

6. "CAN I AFFORD THIS?" EMI CHECKER
• Pre-Purchase Calculator: Check if you can safely afford gadgets or big purchases on monthly EMI before buying.
• Clear Verdict: Instantly gives a Green Light (Safe), Yellow (Caution), or Red (Dangerous) based on your income.
• Real Budget Impact: Shows the exact monthly installment, interest cost, and how much it reduces your daily spending allowance.
• Flexible Terms: Supports 0% No-Cost EMI, Pay Later schemes, and standard 3- to 18-month tenures with built-in presets (iPhone, PS5, Laptop).

7. INVESTMENTS & SAVINGS HUB
• Curated 20% Roadmap: Practical guidance on splitting your savings:
  - 45% Core Wealth: Nifty 50 Index Fund for low-cost, steady growth.
  - 35% High Growth: Flexi-Cap mutual funds for capital expansion.
  - 20% Capital Defense: Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) for tax-free inflation safety.
• 1-Click SIP Copier: Copy your calculated monthly SIP plan to your clipboard with one tap.
• Live Market Benchmarks: Real-time price tracking for Nifty 50, Sensex, 24K Gold, US S&P 500 ETF, and 10-Year Govt Bonds.

8. SPENDING REPORT & INSIGHTS
• Pacing Health Score: A 0–100 score showing your monthly spending discipline.
• Burn Velocity Meter: Tracks how fast you are spending compared to your safe target.
• Month-End Forecast: Predicts your final balance and checks if your 20% savings will remain 100% untouched.

9. INTERACTIVE ONBOARDING & GUIDED TOUR
• 5-Step Guided Tour: Interactive spotlight tour explaining core features to first-time users in simple language.
• Tour Replay & App Reset: Restart the walkthrough or reset app data anytime from settings.

10. APP EXPERIENCE & PRIVACY
• Progressive Web App (PWA): Install on Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac with an app icon on your home screen or desktop.
• Offline-First: Works completely without internet; all data is saved securely on your device.
• Cloud Sync: Optional Google Sign-In to back up and sync your data across devices.
• Dark & Light Modes: Minimalist OLED dark mode and clean light mode.

11. RESET AMOUNT LEFT & PAYDAY AUTO-INCREMENT ENGINE (NEW)
• One-Tap Provision to Reset Amount Left: Set your safe spending pool anytime to match your actual cash in hand or bank balance.
• Pre-Payday Awaiting Salary Mode: If salary hasn't arrived yet before payday (e.g., 5th before 7th), reset amount left to ₹0 or current pocket cash so the app doesn't assume you have money you haven't received yet.
• Payday Arrival & Auto-Increment Protection: When payday arrives on the 7th, prevents unspent previous cash from auto-incrementing beyond your monthly salary.
• Clean Payday Reset: Starts each monthly cycle with exactly your 1-month take-home pay, keeping budgets clean and preventing balance inflation.
• Live Dynamic Recalculation: Instantly recomputes your daily spending allowance across remaining days until payday based on calibrated cash.
• Revert Anytime: Revert back to automatic budget calculation with one tap whenever desired.
