Install Traxpens App

Install Traxpens directly to your desktop or mobile home screen. It runs like a native app with offline capability, fast startup, and no browser address bar clutter.

Browser-specific installation:
💻 Chrome / Edge / Brave (PC & Mac)
Look at the right end of the URL address bar and click the Install icon (💻 or ➕), or open the browser menu (⋮) → "Save and share" → "Install Traxpens" (or "Create shortcut" with Open as window checked).
📱 Android (Chrome)
Tap the three dots (⋮) at the top right of Chrome, then tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
🍏 iPhone & iPad (Safari)
Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow) in the Safari bottom bar, scroll down the actions, and select "Add to Home Screen".
⚠️ Seeing "Already Installed" or "No App Found"?
If you previously deleted a desktop shortcut, Chrome still keeps the app registered in its internal list. Open a new tab, type chrome://apps, right-click Traxpens and choose "Remove from Chrome", then return here and refresh to reinstall fresh!
